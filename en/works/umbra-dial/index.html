Before doors open

Fit the dome.

We need level indoor space, a nearby outlet and step-free load-in. The venue confirms clear exits and its accessibility arrangements.

Compact dome

24 per screening

7 × 7 m clear floor and 3.8 m height. One dedicated grounded 110 V / 15 A circuit.

Hall dome

36 per screening

9 × 9 m clear floor and 5 m height. Two dedicated grounded 110 V / 15 A circuits.

Touring kit: inflatable dome, fulldome projector and surround sound. Allow 60 minutes before the first screening for setup and 45 minutes after the last for strike.
