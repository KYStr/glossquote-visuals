Object studies

Folded ring

A shallow curve runs around the face, while a narrow plane folds into the interior. Turn the ring and watch the highlight cross from the outer edge to the inner one.

Still orb

This silhouette suggests a drop just before it falls. Its curved surface lets the reflection travel continuously, so the highlight seems to move faster than the object.

Open arc

The curve narrows toward each end. Turn it and one tip catches the light while the other stays dark, making the opening easier to see.
