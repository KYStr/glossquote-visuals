Finishes

Light follows the curve.

Each curve begins at a level foot. Mirror polish keeps reflections crisp, while brushed satin diffuses light along its fine grain.

Wipe with a soft cloth. Follow the grain on brushed surfaces and avoid abrasive cleaners.

Delivery and inquiries

Your questions

Which finish suits my space?

Choose mirror polish for crisp reflections in daylight or brushed satin for a softer sheen. The product view lets you compare both finishes.

How would the pieces be delivered?

The collection is designed for individual packaging and parcel delivery. Packaging, shipping and lead times would be confirmed by the retailer. Indicative totals cover the pieces alone.

Can I combine different pieces in one inquiry?

Yes. Each finish gets its own line, and you can edit quantities in the list. Your summary includes the piece, finish, quantity, indicative unit price and total.
