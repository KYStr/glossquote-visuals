Print specs and pricing

  • Allow 5–7 working days for A3 or 7–9 for A2.
  • All designs use 170 gsm uncoated stock. Warm white is standard. Oat adds NT$5 per copy.
  • A3 setup is NT$950 plus NT$22 per copy. A2 setup is NT$1,350 plus NT$32 per copy.
  • Runs of 151 or more add 2 working days. Lead time starts when the print file is approved.

Concept print service. Events are fictional, prices and lead times are illustrative, and print sheets are for offline planning.
