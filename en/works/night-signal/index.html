      Before you go

      When can I enter, and is there an age limit?

      Full-night admission starts at 18:30. Single-set admission starts 15 minutes before the selected set. The concept event is 18+. Any real edition would publish its own admission and ID requirements.

      What about seating, volume and lighting?

      The room is standing-only at ground level, with bass and stage beams. You can bring earplugs or take a break at the back. Accessibility facilities depend on the venue for any real edition.

      What if the event is cancelled or rescheduled?

      The proposed policy is a full refund for cancellation and a choice of transfer or refund for rescheduling. This page only creates an itinerary summary and has no transactions to refund.
