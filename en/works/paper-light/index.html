Surface

Head-on, the paper looks almost uniformly white. Light from the side picks out raised fibers and little dips in a surface that seemed flat. Those differences give the sheet its texture before any ink touches it.

Incidence

Turn the light. Look first at the shadow below the crease, then at the highlight as it crosses the edge. Try Honey or Overcast. The crease holds its place while the white around it changes.

Material notes

The fibers, folds and shadows are original vector artwork. WebGPU layers moving light and fine grain over the sheet. The still version retains the angle and color controls.
