Paper & finishes

Details you can feel.

Stock sets the feel of a piece. The finish decides where the light catches. Start with the way it should feel in someone’s hand.

Cotton stock · 350 g/m²
Soft, thick and receptive to blind debossing. A good fit for business cards and tactile impressions.
Warm-white stock · 300 g/m²
A smooth surface for fine text and one-colour linework. Suited to invitations and inserts.
Recycled stock · 300 g/m²
Natural variation in visible fibres and tone. Suited to belly bands, tags and small gift cards.

Blind debossing presses a design into the sheet without ink. Raking light reveals its depth. One-colour printing keeps text and details easy to read.

Common questions

Can I start without artwork?

Yes. Start with the collection, quantity and purpose. You can bring design references, dimensions and artwork to a future enquiry.

How should I prepare print files?

Supply a print-ready PDF. Keep text and artwork at least 3 mm inside the trim and add 3 mm of bleed to artwork that runs to the edge. Supply a separate vector layer or file for blind debossing.

Can I request a different quantity, size or deadline?

Yes. Each collection lists its typical starting quantity and lead time. Small runs, custom sizes, rush jobs and runs over 200 need a separate proofing and production estimate.

Does downloading the brief place an order?

The download creates a text file you can keep or share with a printer later. This studio concept does not take orders.
