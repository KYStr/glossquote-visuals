How we work.

  1. Define the brief

    We map your audience, current materials and launch constraints. You get a written scope, schedule and fee before work starts.

  2. Make a direction tangible

    We review the direction in its intended context, from print samples to timing tests and browser prototypes. Two rounds of refinement follow.

  3. Hand it over

    We deliver editable source files, export formats and a short walkthrough, so your team can keep using the system.

Before we start.

Can we start with an existing identity?

Yes. We use the existing guidelines and files to identify what carries over, then scope only the missing elements.

What changes the estimate?

More applications, additional languages, custom illustration and production support change the scope. The ranges below each service cover the listed deliverables. Licensing and third-party costs are quoted separately.

How quickly can a project begin?

The lead times shown are production windows after scope approval and receipt of assets. We confirm a start date and review milestones before signing.

Who owns the working files?

The agreed package includes the editable sources listed in your scope after final payment. Any external type or media licenses are itemized before approval.
