    Care and delivery

    How do I clean a sample?

    Dust with a soft cloth, wipe with a little water, then dry. Avoid abrasives on etched and textured faces. Follow your supplier’s care instructions for the finished glass.

    Will the finished glass match the sample?

    Samples help you compare surfaces and light transmission. The finished dimensions, thickness and colours behind the glass affect its appearance. Confirm these details with your fabricator.
