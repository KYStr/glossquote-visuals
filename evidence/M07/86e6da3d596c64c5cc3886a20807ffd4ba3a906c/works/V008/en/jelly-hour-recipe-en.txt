Jelly Hour | Fruit jelly gift box recipe

Mandarin jelly: 3 × 100 mL
Raspberry jelly: 1 × 100 mL
White guava jelly: 2 × 100 mL
Total: 6 cups × 100 mL = 600 mL

Ingredients
Mandarin jelly — Mandarin juice, water, cane sugar, citrus pectin, lemon juice.
Raspberry jelly — Strained raspberry juice, water, cane sugar, citrus pectin, lemon juice.
White guava jelly — White guava juice, water, cane sugar, citrus pectin, lime juice.

Allergens
The concept recipes use no added milk, eggs, peanuts or tree nuts. Shared equipment may come into contact with milk, peanuts and tree nuts.

Storage
Refrigerate and follow the use-by date on the product label.

A seasonal fruit jelly brand concept. The recipe is created in this page for offline use and sharing.
