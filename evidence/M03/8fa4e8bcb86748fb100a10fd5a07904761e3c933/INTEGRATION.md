## Limits

Software GPU evidence does not establish physical-device FPS, power/battery performance or all graphics drivers. The 30fps/DPR goals are not claimed as universal public-SDK caps. The accessibility checks are not a complete human screen-reader audit, and 200% root text sizing is not browser page zoom. Natural frames are sampled motion, not continuous video or measured display FPS. Pages uses meta CSP, not security response headers it does not provide. Concept brands and illustrative venue/product claims do not represent real commercial transactions or conversion results. Original M01/M02 evidence remains historical.
